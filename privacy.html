Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Airlie Scooter Hire ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website and rent a moped from us, how we use it, and how we keep it safe. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Who we are

Airlie Scooter Hire provides 50cc moped rentals in Airlie Beach, Queensland. If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, you can contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

When you submit a booking request or enquiry through our website, we may collect:

  • Your full name and date of birth
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your current address while renting (e.g. hotel or Airbnb)
  • Driver's licence details (licence number, country/state of issue, type, and expiry)
  • Driving history you disclose (at-fault accidents, driving restrictions)
  • The number of riders, your requested rental dates, and your digital signature and declarations

We only collect this information because it is reasonably necessary to assess your rental request, meet our legal and insurance obligations, and provide the rental service.

How we use your information

  • To process and respond to your booking request or enquiry
  • To verify eligibility to ride a 50cc moped and satisfy insurance requirements
  • To arrange pickup, delivery, and drop-off
  • To contact you about your booking
  • To meet our legal obligations, including in the event of an accident, fine, or damage

We do not use your information for marketing without your consent, and we do not sell your personal information.

How your information is handled and shared

When you submit a form on our website, the details are delivered to us by email through our email delivery provider, EmailJS. Our website also uses Google reCAPTCHA to protect our forms from spam and abuse; your use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We do not otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties, except where required or authorised by law (for example, to police, insurers, or transport authorities in connection with an accident, fine, or damage).

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access or disclosure. Booking information is received by email and retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. To make a request, please contact us using the details below. You may also raise a concern with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date shown above.
